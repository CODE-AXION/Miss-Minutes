Record, and get on with your day. The notes show up a few minutes after the meeting ends.

Built-in meeting recorder

Record any screen or window with the call's audio and your mic, with no OBS needed. Start from the header, the tray or Ctrl+Alt+R; mute your mic with Ctrl+Alt+M.

Floating REC toolbar

While you're in Zoom, Teams or Meet, a small always-on-top pill shows the time with Mute and Stop. It stays out of the recording itself.

Notes, written for you

Each recording becomes a Markdown note: overview, topics, decisions, action items and diagrams. Click any [09:12] timestamp to play the recording from that moment.

An organised library

Notes are filed by category and tags. Filter, group, edit, reprocess, or delete meetings, one or many at once, to the Recycle Bin.

Search by word or by meaning

Full-text search finds the exact passage. Optional smart search answers questions like "when did we drop the manual approval step?", running entirely on your PC.

Quiet, in the tray

Starts with Windows and works in the background. Failures are retried on their own, and a notification tells you when notes are ready.

Updates itself

New versions download in the background. Restart to install, or it installs the next time you quit. Never mid-recording.

Your data stays yours

The library is plain Markdown and JSON on your PC. Recordings are only sent to your own Google AI Studio account to write the notes.

How it works

From "Stop recording" to a filed note, without touching anything.

  1. 1

    Detect

    A recording made in the app is queued at once. Files from OBS or other recorders are picked up once they stop growing, so nothing is grabbed mid-recording.

  2. 2

    Compress

    HandBrake (included) shrinks the video, often to 20–40% of its size, so the upload is quick.

  3. 3

    Generate

    A hidden Chrome, signed in to your Google account, uploads the video to AI Studio and asks Gemini for the notes. You never see a window.

  4. 4

    File

    The note is saved under its category, the library index is updated, and a Windows notification says it's ready.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • Google Chrome. The setup wizard checks for it and links to the download if it's missing.
  • A Google account that can use Google AI Studio
  • That's it: Python, HandBrake and the recorder are built in.

Install in a couple of minutes

  1. Run the installer. It installs for your user only, with no admin rights needed.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise it.
  3. Follow the setup wizard. It checks Chrome, signs you in to Google, picks your library and recordings folders, and can start Miss Minutes with Windows.
  4. Record a meeting. Click Record a meeting, use the tray, or press Ctrl+Alt+R. Notes appear a few minutes after you stop.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

The app is a free download. It writes notes through your own Google account in AI Studio, so whatever limits Google sets for your account apply.

Where do my recordings and notes go?

Notes live in your library, Documents\Miss Minutes by default: plain Markdown and JSON you can back up or open anywhere. Recordings stay in your recordings folder (your Videos folder by default). The only thing that leaves your PC is the compressed video, uploaded to your own Google AI Studio account.

I already record with OBS. Can I keep using it?

Yes. Point Miss Minutes at the folder OBS saves to, and every new recording is picked up. You can also drag any video onto the dashboard to process it first.

Does smart search send my notes anywhere?

No. Smart search is optional; when you turn it on it downloads a 207 MB model once and runs entirely on your PC. Keyword search works without it.

Will muting in Miss Minutes mute me in Zoom or Teams?

No, the two are separate. Muting in Miss Minutes only silences your mic in the recording; the screen and the call's audio keep recording. Use headphones so the other people aren't recorded twice.

What happens if my Google session expires?

The video goes back to the queue without using up an attempt, and you get a notification. Click it, sign in, and processing carries on by itself.

How do I uninstall it?

Start menu → Uninstall Miss Minutes. It asks whether to also delete your library and your settings; both default to No, so your notes are kept unless you say otherwise. Your original recordings are never deleted.
